# Tejal Srivastava — Portfolio: design system (A–F) and gaps (G–H)

## A. Page structure (14 A4 pages)
01 Cover · 02 About · 03 Design approach · 04 Selected work
05–06 Degree Show · 07–08 MKS Website · 09–10 Smart Attendance · 11–12 Be Your Own Barista
13 AI + design · 14 Closing
Each case study = 2 pages: (1) hero, context, problem/research, my role, one large visual; (2) architecture, concepts or visual direction, outcome and reflection, one large visual. Original section numbers are kept in merged block titles (e.g. 05–08).

## B. System
Numbered blocks (the brief's section numbers), dashed teal evidence slots, a process line at the foot of every case-study page (Research → Insights → Opportunities → Ideation → Prototyping → Testing → Iteration → Outcome) with the stages covered on that page highlighted. Rules: hairlines not cards, no shadows, no gradients, square corners, one accent.

## C. Typography
Inter Tight (display, titles, tight tracking) + Inter (body) + IBM Plex Mono (metadata, captions, folios). Scale: cover 27mm/14vw · case title 60px · block title 20px · body 14px · meta 11px.

## D. Colour
Paper #F3F0E8 · Ink #1A1C1C · Deep teal #0E5A57 · Muted #6B6F6C · Hairline #D8D3C6 · Sakura #E8B4BC (falling petals and active process-stage dots only; hidden in print). Teal is used only for numbers, active process stages and evidence slots.

## E. Grid
12-column feel on a 1200px max width; A4 print margin 14mm. Blocks sit in 3-, 2- or 1-column grids depending on the case study.

## F. Variation within one system
01 Degree Show — 3 columns, image-led (brand/visual).
02 MKS — 2 columns, product-screen-led.
03 Smart Attendance — 1 column rows: text left, diagram right (system/hardware).
04 Barista — 2 columns, first block full-width for journeys and the blueprint (service).

## G. Missing from your current material
Degree Show: the challenge, sitemap, screenshots, mobile screens, poster/brochure images, reflection. MKS: research findings, personas, journey maps, PRD extract, wireframes, Figma and final screens, reflection. Smart Attendance: problem/user context, sketches, circuit and enclosure photos, flow diagram, testing evidence. Barista: observation photos, interview quotes, pain points, journey maps, blueprint, prototype visuals. General: your LinkedIn and portfolio URLs, languages, and one real example of your AI use.

## H. Internship advice (UX / Product / Service Design)
1. Put a one-line contribution statement on every group project page, and keep to what you did.
2. Lead with Barista and MKS: they show research → synthesis → design.
3. Replace every placeholder with real evidence before adding words; photos of sketches and prototypes beat polish.
4. Show at least one iteration (what changed and why) per project.
5. Keep the PDF under ~15 MB and pair it with a live URL.
